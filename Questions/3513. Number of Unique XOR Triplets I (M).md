### Notes:
- Think of this as a math problem.  
- For each bit of the maximum number, the array will always has some number with that bit is 1 and 0.  Hence the answer should be 2 ** ((math.log(max(nums)), 2) + 1)  e.g. [1,2,3,4,5] -> 5 -> 101 -> 2 ** (2+1) = 8
